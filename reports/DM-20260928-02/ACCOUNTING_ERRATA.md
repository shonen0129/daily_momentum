# SN1_H1 sleeve-cost attribution correction

This correction applies to the Long/Short sleeve decomposition and side-level
Net contribution tables generated by the fixed structural diagnostic. It does
not change the total portfolio P/L or the official evaluator's cost calculation.

The diagnostic allocated each stock-day's full turnover cost according to the
sign of its current weight. When a Long or Short position moved to zero, the
current weight was neutral, so its closing cost was omitted from both sleeve
totals. A sign change also failed to allocate the closing and opening exposure
changes to their respective sleeves. The saved daily account files show that
Long Net plus Short Net exceeded total Net by 0.02831 annualized percentage
points in Train and 0.03616 points in previously viewed historical Valid.

The original figures and total-account results remain archived as produced.
The diagnostic code now calculates turnover separately on positive and
negative portfolio exposures. Entry, exit, and sign-flip costs are assigned to
the sleeve whose exposure changes, with target-missing rows following the
official evaluator's missing-label behavior. Reconciliation is asserted both
row by row and by day.

This code correction was checked with synthetic entry, exit, neutral, sign-flip,
and missing-label cases. The prior Train or historical-Valid account was not
recomputed; historical Valid remains viewed development data.
