# C3S Freeze

Strategy ID: `DM-20260924-C3S-v1`
Frozen artifact: `dm_c3s_submission.zip`

C3S is frozen as a separate research candidate after its Train-only correctness and descriptive screen. The underlying Train period was already known during definition correction, so Train metrics are descriptive and were not used to choose among alternatives. H0 is the exact frozen `DM-20260908-v1` trial `M_res60s1_a0.25`.

Freeze completed before the C3S Valid comparison. The shared Valid period was previously accessed for `DM-20260912-01-v1`; therefore the new comparison is not independent OOS evidence. The candidate, baseline, features, formula, cost convention and evaluation method are fixed. No changes are permitted based on this comparison.

Only `raw_return_1day`, `beta_1day`, `topix_return_1day`, and raw `High` / `Close` / dated `AdjustmentFactor` are read by the submission. No targets are read in prediction.
